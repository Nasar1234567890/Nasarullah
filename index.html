How to take a test

  1. Type your name, choose your class or General Knowledge, then choose a test.
  2. Tap an option (A, B, C or D) to select your answer.
  3. Use Next and Previous to move between questions.
  4. You have 10 minutes. If the time runs out, the test submits automatically.
  5. Press Submit Test when you are done. Your score out of 20 appears at once, with a review of every question.
  6. Your correct answers are shown only after you submit — not during the test.
