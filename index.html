How to take a test

  1. Type your name, choose your class, then a subject, then a test. (General Knowledge opens its Sindhi and English versions first.)
  2. Tap an option (A, B, C or D) to select your answer.
  3. Use Next and Previous to move between questions.
  4. Each test shows its own time limit (10 minutes for most tests, 60 minutes for GK 1st Test). If the time runs out, the test submits automatically.
  5. Press Submit Test when you are done. Your score appears at once, with a review of every question.
  6. In Test mode, your correct answers are shown only after you submit — not during the test. In Learn mode, the correct answer is shown right after each question.
